The work

Built Things will design, fabricate, finish and install frameless inset cabinetry for two rooms: 24 linear feet of kitchen perimeter, a nine-foot island, a plaster hood surround with open shelving, and a twelve-foot pantry wall with glazed uppers. Forty-one boxes in all.

Everything is built on maple-ply carcasses with quartersawn oak face frames, hand-sanded, and sprayed in a two-part conversion varnish in the "Rye" stain you approved on August 14. Shop drawings come to you for sign-off before a single board is cut.

Materials and finishes

BoxesThree-quarter-inch maple plywood, dadoed and glued, with prefinished natural interiors.
Face framesQuartersawn white oak, 3/4 by 1-1/2 inch. Rift grain set aside for the island.
Doors and frontsInset shaker with a 5/8-inch reveal and a 3/32-inch gap. Pantry uppers in seeded glass.
DrawersSolid maple, dovetailed, 5/8-inch sides on Blum Tandem undermount slides, soft close.
FinishTwo-part conversion varnish in a satin sheen. Color "Rye", sample approved August 14.
HardwareEmtek "Alexander" pulls in unlacquered brass, carried as an allowance below.

Terms

Validity. Pricing holds 30 days from the date on this page. Lumber and hardware costs move, so a later start may be repriced.

Schedule. Eleven weeks from approved drawings to install, assuming the site is ready. We confirm the install week four weeks out.

Changes. Anything that changes after drawing approval is written up as a change order with cost and schedule impact, and needs your signature before we proceed.

Site conditions. Walls out of plumb beyond a quarter inch over eight feet may need added scribe work, quoted before we do it.

Storage. Finished casework held more than two weeks past the confirmed install date is billed at $150 per week.

Warranty. Five years on joinery and hardware function, two years on finish, from substantial completion. Normal wood movement is excluded.
