Same data layer as capacity and /schedule — this view writes who and which week; the hours are the job's engineering estimate. Block width = hours, so a full lane LOOKS full. The week cell turns red when a lane is over that week's hours (Evan, this week); a red right edge means production is scheduled at or before that week — engineering is the thing holding it. Green blocks have tracked time ("7h in"). Thanksgiving week shows reduced capacity (24h) straight from the holiday calendar. The dept switcher (▾) turns this into an Assembly board the day that bottleneck wins.
