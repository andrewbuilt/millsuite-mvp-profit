Same visual language as the production swimlane, one level finer: columns are DAYS, blocks stack so small jobs share a day, and the day cell tints red past its hours (Evan Monday, 10/8). The priority rail is the input — reorder it and each lane repacks forward, which is where every job's drawings-by date comes from (the green chip rides the job's LAST block; red = production is already waiting). Hours per day per person come from the roster; one data layer with capacity and /schedule, as before.
